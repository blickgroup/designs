What you're looking for: the timestamp ("Updated 8 mins ago") and the table status cells should read as quieter than the body but still readable at a glance. L1 is the current spec — too close to body. L4 starts to look disabled. My pick: L3 (#8a8a8a) — clearly secondary, not disabled, still readable on both #fff and #fafafa.

Note: whatever you pick here updates --text-muted in themes.css light theme + the spec quick-ref in hub-app.DESIGN.md §9. The dark theme already uses #707070 (which works against the dark canvas) — no change there.
